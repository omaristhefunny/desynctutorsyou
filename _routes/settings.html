Safety
Moderation and blocking

Public rooms are checked automatically before a message is shown to anyone. Direct messages are never sent to moderation — they are private, and unwanted DMs are handled by blocking instead.

Blocked people
Blocking is managed from a person's name in chat. A block hides their messages and stops their DMs reaching you.
