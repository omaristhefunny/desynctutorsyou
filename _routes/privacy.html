What desync is

desync is a privacy-oriented access tool: a browser-like interface with optional proxy/relay networking, optional account features, and optional entertainment catalogs, supported by advertising so it can stay free. Its purpose is to help people reach information and tools when ordinary paths are restricted by network filters or similar technical barriers — not to promote breaking the law or any institution's rules.

Alternate links and mirrors exist so the service can be found when a route is blocked. That does not mean we authorize or take responsibility for violating any school, employer, library, government, or network acceptable-use policy. You remain responsible for every rule that applies to you. We do not guarantee anonymity or that any filter or monitoring system won't notice your use.

desync is for people 13 years of age or older. You must be at least 13 to use the site, and you confirm you are 13 or older when you create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has given us personal information or created an account, email us at the address below and we will delete the account and its data.

Information we collect

  • Account: Your email address, username, password (stored only as a secure hash), the date you confirmed you are 13 or older, your plan, and when you last logged in.
  • D1SC00RD Your D1SC00RD user ID and username is collected to provide you the plan based on your server roles.
  • What you post: Chat messages in public rooms, direct messages, groups, friend requests and blocks. Public chat can be seen by other users, so don't share personal information there.
  • AI features: The prompts you send to AI and their replies is something we may possibly collect. We mainly count daily AI usage as analytical data and apply limits.
  • Saves and library: We provide save data for games, music libraries, and preferences for the website.
  • Technical information: Your IP address, browser and device information, and data stored in your browser (local storage and cookies), such as your login session. We use your IP address for security, rate limits and abuse prevention, including daily AI limits for people who aren't logged in.
  • Payments: All subscription payments are managed and handled by Stripe. We keep your subscription status and customer ID. We never ask or collect credit card numbers.
  • Web: Any pages you open through the web passes through the wisp provider and bare. We do not keep record of pages you visit. We only keep a temporary connection log which includes your IP address for security reasons.
