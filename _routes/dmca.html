How to report infringement

If you believe copyrighted material is hosted on, embedded in, or linked from desync, send us a notice at the address below. We will review valid claims and may disable the specific references under our control.

Email contact@desynctutoring.com

What we can act on

desync is a general-purpose browser/proxy with catalogs that discover or request third-party content. For movies, music and some games, we do not host the underlying files — they come from separate third-party sources. A valid notice should identify the precise desync URL, catalog entry, or page under our control that you want disabled. For files hosted only on a third-party origin, please also notify that origin's operator.

What to include in your notice

To be valid, your notice should contain substantially the following:

  • Signature: your physical or electronic signature (a typed full legal name is acceptable by email), as the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed or someone authorized to act for them.
  • The work: identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or a representative list if several works are covered by one notice.
  • The material: identification of the material claimed to be infringing and information reasonably sufficient for us to locate it (direct URLs, catalog titles, file names).
  • Contact information: your address, telephone number, and email address.
  • Good-faith statement: a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • Accuracy statement: a statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner of the exclusive right or authorized to act on their behalf.

Counter-notice

If you believe material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the same address with your contact information, identification of the material and where it appeared, and a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe the removal was a mistake.

Repeat infringers

In appropriate circumstances we may disable catalog entries, remove hosted modules, and terminate the accounts of users who are repeat infringers.
