Terms & Privacy Policy

Who can use desync, what we collect, and how we use it.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Age requirement: 13 and older

desync is for people 13 years of age or older. You must be at least 13 to use the site, and you confirm you are 13 or older when you create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has given us personal information or created an account, email us at the address below. We will delete the account and its data.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address, username, password (stored only as a secure hash), the date you confirmed you are 13 or older, your plan, and when you last logged in.
  • Discord (only if you link it): your Discord user ID and username, used to give you the plan your server roles include.
  • What you post: chat messages in public rooms, direct messages, groups, friend requests and blocks. Public chat can be seen by other users, so don't share personal information there.
  • AI features: the prompts you send and the AI's replies. If you use the screen assistant, it sends a screenshot of what you choose to share with each question. We also count daily AI usage to apply limits.
  • Saves and library: game save data, music favorites, playlists and site preferences.
  • Technical information: your IP address, browser and device information, and data stored in your browser (local storage and cookies), such as your login session. We use your IP address for security, rate limits and abuse prevention, including daily AI limits for people who aren't logged in.
  • Payments: if you buy a plan, payment is handled by Stripe. We keep your subscription status and Stripe customer ID, never your card number.
  • Web proxy: pages you open through the proxy pass through our servers and a VPN provider. We do not keep a record of the pages you visit; we keep short-lived connection logs (which include your IP address) for security.

How we use your information

To run the site and your account, deliver messages, run the AI features, keep games and music in sync across devices, apply plan limits, prevent spam and abuse, keep the service secure, and understand how the site is used so we can improve it. We send email only for account purposes, such as sign-up and password reset codes.

We never sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help run desync, and only what each one needs:

  • Groq processes AI prompts, screen-assistant screenshots and chat moderation.
  • Google Analytics measures site usage using cookies (Google's privacy policy).
  • Advertising partners show ads on the site and may use cookies or similar technologies.
  • Discord, if you link your account, to check your server roles and send you codes.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Our email provider delivers verification and password-reset codes.
  • Our VPN provider carries web proxy traffic.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the service.

How long we keep it

Account data, messages, saves and your music library are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Daily AI usage records are deleted after about 31 days. Proxy connection logs and other security logs are kept only briefly.

Deleting your account and your rights

You can delete your account at any time from the Account page (Delete account). This permanently removes your account and the data tied to it: messages and DMs, friends, groups you own, game saves, music library and AI history. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it.

Security

We protect your information with reasonable security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and hashed passwords. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.
